Talk to Mingos

Mingos is the AI voice agent from the talk. It takes coffee orders for a pretend campus café. Order something, interrupt it, ask it odd questions, and listen for the pause before each reply.

How to start

  1. Tap Talk to Mingos at the bottom right of the page.
  2. Allow the microphone when your browser asks.
  3. Wait for “Hi, this is Mingos!” and speak normally. Tap End call when you’re done.
